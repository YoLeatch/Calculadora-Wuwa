The Damage Formula

The simplified damage formula is presented as follows:

Simplified Damage Formula
$$\text{Damage} = \text{Base DMG} \times \text{Resistances} \times \text{Bonuses}$$

1.1 The Resistances

Resistances are based on the receiver and are multiplied with each other.

Total Resistance Formula
$$\text{Res}_{Tot} = \text{Def}\% \times \text{DmgRedux} \times \text{ElemRes}$$

1.1.1 Elemental Resistance

Calculated as the sum of Base Resistance and Resistance Penetration.

Elemental Resistance
$$\text{Res}_{Tot} = \text{Res}_{\text{Base}} - \text{Res PEN}_{\text{Attacker}}$$

The effective resistance multiplier is calculated non-linearly:

  • If $Res_{Total} < 0$: $1 - \frac{Res_{Total}}{2}$
  • If $0 \le Res_{Total} < 0.8$: $1 - Res_{Total}$
  • If $Res_{Total} \ge 0.8$: $\frac{1}{1 + 5 \times Res_{Total}}$

1.1.2 Defense (Def%)

Defense reduction applies to the enemy stats before entering the formula. The result can be max 200%.

Enemy Defense Formula
$$\text{Enemy Defense} = 792 + 8 \times \text{Level}_{\text{Enemy}}$$
Def% Formula
$$\text{Def}\% = \frac{800 + \text{Level}_{\text{Attacker}} \times 8}{\text{Defense}_{\text{Target}} \times (1 - \text{Def Ignore}_{\text{Attacker}}) + 800 + \text{Level}_{\text{Attacker}} \times 8}$$

1.1.3 Reduction

Elemental and Damage reductions work independently using the formula:

$$1 - (\text{Base} + \text{Bonus})$$

1.2 Scaling

Base Damage Formula
$$\text{Base DMG} = (\text{Attr} \times \text{MV}\% \times \text{Innate DMG}\%) + \text{Flat Damage} + \text{Flat Bonus}$$
📸
Snapshot Abilities snapshot stats/buffs at the time of casting.

1.3 Bonuses

Bonuses are multiplicative in the formula:

Bonuses Formula
$$\text{Bonuses} = \text{Crit Multiplier} \times \text{Damage Bonus}\% \times \text{Special Damage}\% \times \text{DMG Amplify}$$

1.3.1 Crit Multiplier

Base stats: 5% Rate, 150% Damage

  • Non-Crit: 1
  • Crit: Crit Damage%
  • Average: $1 + \text{Crit Rate}\% \times (\text{Crit Damage}\% - 1)$

1.3.2 Damage Bonus

$$\text{Damage}\% = 1 + \text{General}\% + \text{Element}\% + \text{Type}\%$$

Types include: Resonance Skill, Normal Attack, Heavy Attack, Liberation, Intro, Outro, Echoes

1.3.4 Damage Amplify

Recognized by the keyword "Deepen". It acts as a final multiplicative layer.

$$\text{DMG Amplify Tot} = 1 + (\text{Target Amplify} + \text{Attacker Amplify})$$

Toughness (Hardness)

Toughness damage stuns the enemy when depleted.

Lv 90 Scaling: 262x Level 1 values.

Hardness Damage Formula
$$\text{Hardness Damage} = ((\text{Dmg Done} \times \text{Hardness}_{\text{Skill}}) + \text{Tough}) \times \text{Modifiers} + \text{Parry}$$

Energy

3.1 Resonance Liberation Energy

Used for Ultimates. Affected by Energy Regen%.

  • Off-field: Generates 50% of on-field energy (unaffected by Regen%)
  • Perfect Dodge: Grants ~3 Energy

3.2 Concerto Energy

Max 100. Used for Intro/Outro skills. NOT affected by Energy Regen%.

3.3 Special Energy

Tied to Forte Circuit (the specific mechanic of each character).

4.3 Substat Optimization Rules (TC Standards)

For theorycrafting calculations:

  1. Max 5 rolls per piece.
  2. Crit Limit: Max 9 rolls total (Rate + Dmg).
  3. Unique Substats: Assumed 12 fixed and 12 fluid substats in a full set.
  4. Values: Assumed "Average" based on median.
